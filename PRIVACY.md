# Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Bili Catfish does not collect, transmit, or sell any user data. / Bili Catfish 不收集、不传输、不出售任何用户数据。

## What the extension stores / 扩展保存什么

Your blocking rules (video IDs, uploader IDs, toggle settings) are saved only in your browser's local storage (`chrome.storage.local`). They never leave your device and are not synced anywhere. / 你的屏蔽规则（视频 ID、UP 主 ID、开关设置）仅保存在浏览器本地（`chrome.storage.local`），不会离开你的设备，也不会同步到任何位置。

## Network requests / 网络请求

The extension makes read-only queries to Bilibili's official endpoint (`https://api.bilibili.com/x/web-interface/view`) to convert video card links into stable IDs for local matching. No user data, rules, or identifiers of your browser are sent with these queries. / 扩展对 Bilibili 官方接口发起只读查询，把视频卡片链接换算成稳定的 ID 用于本地匹配。查询不携带任何用户数据、规则或浏览器标识。

The extension also observes the user's own "not interested" / "cancel" requests (`feedback/dislike`, `feedback/dislike/cancel`) on Bilibili pages to record or remove rules locally. / 扩展在 Bilibili 页面上观察用户自己触发的「不感兴趣 / 撤销」请求，用于在本地记录或解除规则。

## What we do NOT do / 我们不做的事

- No analytics, telemetry, tracking, or remote config. / 无统计、埋点、追踪或远程配置。
- No account system. No login required. / 无账号系统，无需登录。
- No ads. / 无广告。
- No data sharing with third parties. / 不与任何第三方共享数据。

## Contact / 联系方式

Open an issue at https://github.com/StepaniaH/bili-catfish/issues if you have questions about this policy. / 如对本政策有疑问，请在 GitHub 仓库提交 Issue。
